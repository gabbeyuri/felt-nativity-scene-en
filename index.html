Frequently asked questions

Before you decide

Do the patterns arrive printed?
No. You receive PDF files to print at home or at any print shop, on letter or A4 paper.
Will it look exactly like the photo?
The patterns reproduce the style and proportions of the scene. Felt colors, stitches, stuffing and hand-finishing may create small differences. The result will be in the same spirit as the photo, made by your own hands.
What size are the pieces? Do I need to resize the patterns?
Each character's measurements are included in the guide. The patterns already come in real size for standard paper: just print at 100% scale, without "fit to page".
I've never sewn felt before. Can I still do this?
Yes, as long as you're willing to follow a step-by-step PDF. This is a hand-sewing project with simple stitches. The guide covers joining pieces, stuffing and finishing. A sewing machine is not required.
Is there a video course?
No. The package is 100% PDF: patterns + guides. Ideal if you'd rather print and follow along at your own pace, without relying on video.
Are physical materials included?
No. Felt, thread, stuffing and accessories are purchased separately. The ready-made list is included in the materials guide, so you don't make the wrong trip to the store.
Is there enough time to finish it by Christmas?
The sooner you start, the easier the process will be. You can make one piece at a time, at your own pace, until the whole scene is complete.
Do I get it right away? Does it work on my phone?
Yes. After your purchase, you get instant access to the files by email. The PDFs open on phone, tablet or computer.
How does the guarantee work?
Full refund within 7 days of purchase if, after opening the files, you find it isn't what you needed.
